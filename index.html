Projects

Design project · Embedded and IoT · 2026

FLOATech: Arduino-based floating unit for tilapia farms

A solar-powered unit that floats on a fish pond and looks after it automatically. It monitors water parameters in real time, feeds the fish on schedule and runs aeration, so farmers of Oreochromis niloticus (Nile tilapia) spend less time checking the pond by hand.

Built and tested on a real pond by a four-person team at Holy Cross of Davao College.

What went into it

  • Water-parameter sensors with real-time feedback monitoring
  • Automated feeder and water aeration
  • Arduino control with custom-soldered circuit boards, voltage regulators, LCD and keypad
  • Solar panel, charge controller and battery for off-grid power
  • Weatherproof enclosures on a pontoon frame of buoyant barrels
