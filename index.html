02. FLOATech

FLOATech

An Arduino-based, solar-powered floating unit for tilapia farms. It looks after a fish pond automatically, designed so farmers of Oreochromis niloticus (Nile tilapia) spend less time checking the pond by hand. Built and tested on a real pond by a four-person team at Holy Cross of Davao College, 2026.

  1. 01.

    Real-time water monitoring

    Water-parameter sensors with real-time feedback monitoring.

  2. 02.

    Automated feeding

    An automated feeder that feeds the fish on schedule.

  3. 03.

    Water aeration

    Runs water aeration for the pond automatically.

What went into it

  • Arduino control with custom-soldered circuit boards, voltage regulators, LCD and keypad
  • Solar panel, charge controller and battery for off-grid power
  • Weatherproof enclosures on a pontoon frame of buoyant barrels
  • Microbubble diffuser for aeration and a GSM module

Recognized twice in 2026: Second Runner Up, Best Design Project Exhibit (TechnoFair 2026), and 2nd Best Research Poster (CET Research Conference, Conversazione 2026).
